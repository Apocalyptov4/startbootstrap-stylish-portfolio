    Search areas

    Job Radar searches Adzuna, which lists jobs from thousands of employers and job sites, around each of these places. Add keywords to look for one kind of job only, or leave them empty to get everything nearby.

      Job sites

      On the website, these settings are stored in the file job-scraper/sources.json in your GitHub repository. Edit that file to add search areas or companies, or to switch sites on and off. The website picks up the change at its next automatic update.

      Companies to follow

      Paste a company's careers page link (e.g. jobs.lever.co/spotify, boards.greenhouse.io/stripe or jobs.ashbyhq.com/notion), or pick the system and type its name as it appears in that link.
